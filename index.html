Context, problem and goal

Problem

Planning one sortie took 2–3 hours across several legacy tools.

Only Earl knew how it all fit together. He wants to retire…

Goal

Replace the legacy tools with one easy-to-follow workflow: faster, more automated, and always compliant.

And help Earl retire.

Insight

Flight managers aren’t looking for an answer. They’re checking one.

So the question changed

What can we automate?

What do they need to verify, and how fast can we make that?

Five challenges

Five places checking slowed them down.

Challenge 1 of 5

Scattered tools, no documented order

A guided planning mode.

Challenge 2 of 5

Statuses buried in documents

Summarized, with clear statuses.

ClearedNot cleared

Challenge 3 of 5

Every value checked by hand

Every value traces to its source.

Challenge 4 of 5

Automation adds checking

A weight builder, not a weight calculator.

Challenge 5 of 5

No trusted route to check against

Trusted routes kept intact.
